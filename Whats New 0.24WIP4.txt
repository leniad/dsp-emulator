What's New!!! DSP Emulator

DSP 0.24b4 21/11/26
**********
-General
    +Improved the mouse cursor icon. It now displays a crosshair when enabled, even in fullscreen mode.
    +CPU Engine
        -HD6309
            +Separated from the M6809.
            +Fixed timings.
            +Added 8-bit indexed addressing with displacement and fixed 16-bit displacement addressing.
            +Fixed TRF and TRF_EX.
        -HU6280
            +Added functions to read the IRQ and timer status.
            +Fixed timings.
            +Fixed addressing modes.
            +Added opcodes $3,$c,$e,$13,$14,$16,$1c,$1e,$23,$33,$24,$2c,$34,$3c,$89,$36,$2e,$3e,$2a,$42,$56,$4e,$5e,$54,$76,$6e,$7e,$70,$83,$a3,$96,$b6,$be,$b8,$ba,$c0,$c4,$cc,$c3,$d3,$e3,$d6,$e4,$ec,$f4.
            +Fixed opcodes $4,$40,$50,$60.
            +Fixed audio in Tumble Pop, FunkyJet, etc.
        -Konami
            +Separated from the M6809.
            +Fixed timings.
            +Fixed TRF and TRF_REG.
            +Added addressing modes from $a2 to $f6.
            +Fixed numerous opcodes!
        -M6502
            +Added bus cycle counting to the NMOS version.
            +Added opcodes $a3,$a7,$af,$b7,$bf,$c7,$cb.
            +Improved IRQ handling.
        -M68000
            +Fixed IRQ 7 handling.
            +Added functions for external IRQ handling.
            +Fixed timings.
            +Fixed opcodes: mulu, muls, movep.w, movep.l, eori.w tos, negx.l, clr.X, trap, link, ulink, rtr, addq.l, sbcd rr, sbcd mm, suba.w, subx.b mm, subx.l rr, abcd mm, addx.X rr, lsr.X, ror.b, asl.X.
            +Added opcodes nbcd b and roxl.l.
        -M6805
            +Fixed opcodes $38,$39,$40,$48,$49,$58,$59,$a0,$b0,$c0,$a1,$b1,$c1,$d1,$d2,$a3,$b9,$d9,$ab,$bb,$cb,$db,$fb.
        -NEC V20/V30/V33
            +Fixed opcodes: ADCB, ADCW, SBBB, SBBW, SHR_BYTE, SHR_WORD, SHL_BYTE, SHL_WORD, SHRA_WORD, pushf, imul_d8, rotshft_bd8, rotshft_wd8, rotshft_b, rotshft_w, rotshft_bcl, NEG and MUL. (Raiden finally works!)
        -uPD7810
            +Fixed IRQ clearing and jump handling.
            +Fixed timings and opcode sizes.
            +Fixed opcodes XRA_X_A, OFFI_X, ONI_X and LDEAX_Dp.
            +Added opcodes SLR_B, SLR_C, SLL_A, STEAX_Hp, DSLL_EA, TABLE.
        -Z80
            +Fixed opcodes ld A,I, in E,(C) and ld A,R.
            +Fixed DD/FD opcodes, removing all ED prefixes (fixes Dananator).
    +Graphics Engine
        -Fixed scroll and zoom functions.
        -Atari MO
            +Fixed tile rendering.
            +Fixed object traversal and priority handling.
            +Changed the supported number of plane colors.
        -AVG/DVG Engine
            +Fixed the base clock.
            +Fixed the number of vectors.
            +Fixed consumed cycles.
            +Fixed reset handling.
        -DECO BAC06: Fixed scrolling.
        -Taito TC0180VCU: Rewrote the driver; fixed zoom, priorities, etc.
        -Taito K051316: Added graphics rotation and scrolling.
        -Taito K051960
            +Fixed zoom.
            +Added a sprite buffer.
        -Taito K053246/K053247/K055673
            +Fixed zoom.
            +Fixed the number of sprites and color handling.
    +Controls Engine
        -Improved the control assignment system. Up to four different keyboards can now be configured and assigned independently to players. When joysticks are detected, buttons can also be remapped for up to four joysticks and assigned to individual players.
        -Added support for three controllers.
        -Improved joystick input handling by normalizing readings to prevent false movements.
        -Improved keyboard input handling to prevent ghost key presses.
    +Sound Engine
        -Normalized audio output to prevent clipping.
        -Improved the audio system, significantly reducing background noise.
        -Taito Sound
            +Fixed audio in Operation Wolf.
            +Added sound support for Taito System B.
        -N2A03: Rewrote the audio implementation.
        -AY8910
            +Added YM2149 support.
            +Fixed AY8912 outputs.
        -DAC: Normalized output to prevent audio clipping.
        -Flower Audio: Improved output, gain and channel mixing.
        -Game Boy: Rewrote the audio implementation.
        -HuC6280: Added audio emulation.
        -Pokey: Rewrote it to adapt it to Atari consoles.
        -QSound: Updated; it now uses the sample ROM.
        -Samples: Fixed free channel selection.
        -SEGA PCM: Improved resampling and mixing.
        -TMS36XX: Fixed melody playback; all notes now sound separately.
        -uPD7759: Improved channel mixing.
        -YM2610: Added sound chip
        -YM2612: Added sound chip.
    +Miscellaneous
        -Taito C-Chip
            +Separated M68000 access from the ASIC itself.
            +Fixed the IRQ clearing timer.
        -Added X24C01 EEPROM support.
        -VIA6522
            +Fixed timers.
            +Added CA1 support.
        -MOS6526: Rewritten.
        -MOS6566: Rewritten.
